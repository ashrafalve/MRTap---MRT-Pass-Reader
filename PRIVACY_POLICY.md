# Privacy Policy for MRTap

**Last updated:** October 16, 2026

**MRTap — MRT Pass Reader** ("the Application", "we", "our", or "us") is an independent, offline-first mobile utility application designed to read public transit cards (MRT Pass & Rapid Pass) used on Dhaka Metro Rail.

We value your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines our strict policy regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when using our Application.

---

## 1. Information Collection and Use

**MRTap does NOT collect, transmit, store, or share any personal information.**

- **No Personal Data Collected:** We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, device identifiers, IP addresses, location data, or payment information.
- **No User Accounts:** You do not need to register, log in, or provide any credentials to use the Application.
- **No Third-Party Analytics or Trackers:** The Application contains no tracking libraries, advertising SDKs (e.g., Google AdMob), or behavioral analytics services (e.g., Google Analytics, Firebase).

---

## 2. On-Device Card Data & Storage

- **NFC Technology:** When you tap an MRT Pass or Rapid Pass against your device, the Application uses your device's Near Field Communication (NFC) hardware to read publicly accessible transaction records and balance numbers stored directly on the physical card's memory chip (Sony FeliCa).
- **Local Storage Only:** Any scanned card identifiers (IDm) and transaction logs are stored exclusively in a private, encrypted local SQLite database on your device.
- **No Cloud Synchronization:** Your card balance, trip history, and saved cards are never transmitted over the internet or uploaded to any external server or cloud service.

---

## 3. Device Permissions

The Application requests the minimum necessary permissions strictly required to perform its core functions:

- **NFC (`android.permission.NFC`):** Required to read transit cards when held against the device.
- **Internet Permission:** The Application does **NOT** require or request access to the internet. It operates 100% offline.

---

## 4. User Data Control & Deletion

You maintain complete control over your data:

- You can delete any saved card or clear all stored transit records at any time directly within the Application via **Settings → Clear all data**.
- Uninstalling the Application permanently removes all locally stored data from your device.

---

## 5. Children's Privacy

The Application does not collect or solicit any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

---

## 6. Third-Party Links and Affiliation Disclaimer

- **Independent Application:** MRTap is an independent, unofficial open-source application. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or operated by Dhaka Mass Transit Company Limited (DMTCL), Dhaka Transport Coordination Authority (DTCA), or any government authority.
- The Application does not link to external websites or third-party web services.
